CLIP GRABBER
Download just the part of a video you want, not the whole thing.

It looks and works like a website, but it runs on your own computer,
so your downloads never go through anyone else's server.

------------------------------------------------------------------
ONE-TIME SETUP
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Install Python from https://www.python.org/downloads/
   On Windows, tick "Add Python to PATH" on the first setup screen.

------------------------------------------------------------------
START IT
------------------------------------------------------------------
Windows:  double-click  "Start Clip Grabber (Windows).bat"
Mac:      double-click  "Start Clip Grabber (Mac).command"
          (if macOS blocks it: right-click > Open > Open)

Your browser opens the app automatically. The first start takes a
minute or two while it downloads its tools. Each start also checks
for engine updates, which keeps YouTube downloads working.

Keep the small black window open while you use it. Close it, or
click "Quit" in the app, to stop.

------------------------------------------------------------------
USE IT
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Paste a video link and click Load.
2. Pick the part you want:
   - drag the orange handles on the timeline, or
   - type times like 1:30 or 1:02:03, or
   - play the preview and click "Use now" at the right moment, or
   - click a chapter name, if the video has chapters.
3. "Play the clip" previews exactly what you selected.
4. Choose Video MP4 or Audio MP3, and a quality.
5. Click "Download clip".

Clips are saved to:  Downloads > Clip Grabber
Names look like:     Video title [1m30s-2m45s].mp4

"Exact cut" starts the clip exactly at your time. It is a little
slower. Turn it off for speed; the clip may then start a second or
two early.

------------------------------------------------------------------
MAKE IT A REAL APP (Windows .exe, optional)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click "Build EXE (Windows).bat" once. After a few minutes you
get "dist\Clip Grabber.exe". Put it on your Desktop and double-click
it like any other program. No Python window needed after that.

The .exe does not update itself. If downloads start failing a few
months later, run "Build EXE (Windows).bat" again for a fresh copy.

------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
------------------------------------------------------------------
- "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot": click "YouTube sign-in" at the
  top of the app. Pick a browser where you're signed in to YouTube
  (Firefox works best), or export a cookies.txt file with the free
  "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" browser extension and choose it there.
- Downloads suddenly fail: click "Update download engine" at the
  bottom of the app, then restart it. Sites change often.
- Under each clip, "Details" shows exactly what happened.
- Port already in use / app already open: starting it again just
  opens the existing window.

Only download videos you have the right to use.
